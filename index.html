Automating Powder Days

I love skiing. A lot. I went all in in 2021 during COVID. I moved to Colorado and scheduled my winter around getting as many days on the mountain as possible. There was one catch: most resorts required reservation due to social distancing. You could lock in a few days further out but have unlimited reservations within 7 days. These reservations became available at 12:00am every night. If you didn’t book soon after that window opened or very early in the morning, you were out of luck.
